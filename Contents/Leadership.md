Alongside my engineering studies, I have actively taken on leadership, mentoring, and ambassador roles that have strengthened my communication, project management, and stakeholder engagement skills.

These experiences include representing Monash University as a Campus and International Student Ambassador, mentoring first-year students, contributing to the Monash Association of Civil Engineering Students (ACES), and founding the Monash Mauritian Society. Through these roles, I have organised events, secured sponsorship, mentored students, and worked with diverse teams to build stronger communities.

I believe these experiences complement my technical engineering background by developing the leadership and interpersonal skills required to deliver successful engineering projects.
