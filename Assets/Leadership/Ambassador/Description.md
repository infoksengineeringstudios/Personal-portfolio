## Overview

Represented Monash University and the Faculty of Engineering across student recruitment, outreach, orientation, and peer mentoring initiatives. Worked with prospective and commencing students through campus events, webinars, and mentoring programs while promoting Monash's academic opportunities and student experience both locally and internationally.

## My role

As both a Campus Ambassador and International Student Ambassador, I represented Monash University at key recruitment and engagement events. Responsibilities included assisting prospective students, supporting university events, participating in international outreach initiatives, and sharing experiences as an engineering student.

Alongside these ambassador roles, I also served as a Peer Mentor, supporting first-year students as they transitioned into university life.

## Key contributions

- Represented the Faculty of Engineering during Open Day, Orientation, and university events.
- Participated in Monash's International Student Ambassador Program.
- Selected to feature in Monash University's official Africa recruitment campaign.
- Participated in webinars and student information sessions for prospective international students.
- Mentored 17 first-year students, providing guidance on academic success, university life, and settling into Monash.
- Supported student engagement and community building across multiple university initiatives.

## Reflection

These experiences significantly strengthened confidence in public speaking, mentoring, and communicating with people from diverse backgrounds. They also taught how to represent an organisation professionally and reinforced the importance of creating an inclusive environment for new students.
