## Overview

The Rafflesia Vision Valley logistics hub was constrained by limited accessibility through existing underground tunnels, creating concerns regarding future traffic capacity, emergency access, and operational reliability.

This project involved the planning, geometric design, and pavement design of two proposed arterial link roads connecting the logistics hub to Elmina Road and Airiza Road. The designs were developed with a 30-year service life, considering future traffic growth, safety requirements, environmental constraints, and economic feasibility.

## Design Objectives

- Improve accessibility and reliability of the logistics hub
- Support future economic growth through increased transport capacity
- Develop safe and efficient road alignments
- Minimise construction and maintenance costs
- Consider environmental, social, and stakeholder constraints

## My Role & Contributions

I was responsible for the preliminary planning, road alignment development, geometric design, and pavement design of the proposed link roads.

Key tasks included:

- Analysed site constraints including terrain conditions, existing utilities, environmental reserves, and nearby developments
- Developed and evaluated alternative road alignments using a multi-criteria assessment approach
- Created horizontal and vertical road geometry in Autodesk Civil 3D while ensuring compliance with Austroads standards
- Designed road cross-sections, including lane widths, shoulders, medians, drainage considerations, and earthworks requirements
- Completed pavement design assessments and compared alternative pavement structures based on performance and cost
- Produced engineering drawings, design documentation, and cost estimates

## Outcome

The final designs achieved Austroads compliance while balancing safety, operational efficiency, and construction feasibility.

Estimated project costs:

- Elmina Road Link: $23.37 million
- Airiza Road Link: $35.87 million

This project developed practical experience in transport infrastructure design, including road alignment development, Civil 3D modelling, pavement engineering, and applying industry design standards.
