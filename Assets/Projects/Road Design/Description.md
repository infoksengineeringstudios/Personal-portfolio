## Overview

Designed two arterial link roads connecting a logistics hub to Elmina Road and Airiza Road, with a 30-year design life and Austroads compliance.

## Key contributions

- Assessed site constraints including terrain, utilities, heritage, and environmental zones.
- Developed and compared alignment options using a multi-criteria assessment.
- Modelled horizontal and vertical geometry, cross-sections, and pavement structures in Civil 3D.
- Produced engineering drawings, documentation, and cost estimates.

## Outcome

Final designs met Austroads requirements with estimated costs of $23.37M (Elmina link) and $35.87M (Airiza link).
